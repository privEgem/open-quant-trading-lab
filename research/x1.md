# X1: relative survivors, selection effects and stopping

X1 was a separate experimental lane. Its final preserved state was frozen research material; the lane is dormant. A frozen survivor is a relative research outcome, not a proven edge or a promotion decision.

Paths are under `trading_lab/`. K1 remained parked and unresolved; it is not silently promoted or represented as a frozen lead. A/B/D/J were archived as failed, fragile or unfair; K was an archived precursor superseded by the K1 split. M/N/O/P/Q/R were screened but not admitted. Their runners, trial tables and packet archives are excluded; these outcomes remain in this summary rather than being erased.

Seven independent modules preserve useful code without duplicating the inherited bot tree. Packaging removes historical launchers/default-data searches and requires explicit inputs. Signal parameters and execution rules were not optimized. APIs remain different; G uses one-share PnL, L fixed notional, and other references use risk/cash/notional sizing. G/L expect a precomputed `vwap` column. Read `docs/API.md` before comparing them.

F/G/H/I/L tests use small artificial setups and verify concrete behavior. C/E tests verify no-setup rejection; their positive branches have less extraction validation. These checks establish runnable boundaries, not historical result reproduction. Older clock, missing-exit and intrabar assumptions remain visible and should prevent casual use of headline PnL.

The research lesson is selection humility. A family can survive because nearby ideas fail, while still being too sparse, friction-sensitive or regime-dependent to justify deployment. A preserved source ledger, failed-family map and stopping rule are more useful than a flattering winner narrative. This release does not reopen X1, search new variants or revise its historical hierarchy.

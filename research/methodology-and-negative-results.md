# Methodology and negative results

This project's useful output includes software, sharper execution semantics, better evidence discipline and decisions to stop. None establishes a validated profitable strategy. Surviving a local comparison is weaker than demonstrating an independent economic effect after costs.

Separate four statements: code implements a rule; an offline test exercises a behavior; a historical report records an empirical result; an independent study supports a general conclusion. The first three do not imply the fourth. The synthetic quickstarts address engineering only.

Keep feature windows, trading sessions, signal clocks, next-bar fills, exit priority, actual initial-stop risk, costs and missing-data policy explicit. Compare alternatives under matched contracts. Record every attempted variant; a selected survivor without its search history understates selection uncertainty.

Define a falsifier and stopping criteria before viewing held-out evidence. Keep descriptive diagnostics separate from primary criteria. Preserve unfavorable results even when a new explanation sounds appealing. Later sensitivity work needs its own label, trial accounting and authority.

Historical outcomes retained here: S1 unpromoted; X1 only relative survivors; X2 no serious candidate; Bauer–Chernov extension unfavorable. These are bounded results and stopping decisions, not proof that every related idea is useless. They also do not authorize a fresh search.

The public source does not contain enough lawful empirical inputs to repeat those historical studies. A future independent researcher must obtain their own permitted data, define a new study, verify input provenance and point-in-time limitations, freeze methods and track all trials. Do not splice the synthetic demo's numbers into a historical performance narrative.

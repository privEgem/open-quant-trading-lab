# X2: paper review that did not produce a candidate

X2's preserved dormancy decision is the source of this case study. No X2 strategy or code is invented for the release.

A bounded paper-review phase examined validation, overfitting, execution/cost realism and intraday mechanisms. Subsequent triage considered options, dealer gamma, macro/positioning measures, public positioning proxies, regime/time structure and an older parked overnight-inventory dossier. The bounded positioning-proxy feasibility screen found the available directions too delayed, noisy, broad or poorly matched to the intended intraday question to earn near-term continuation.

The result was improved skepticism and research vocabulary, **no serious candidate**, no implementation-worthy hypothesis and dormancy. The older overnight-inventory dossier did not earn reactivation. More paperwork and more possible papers were not treated as evidence that strategy research should keep moving.

Two primary methodological references from the reading set are [Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado and Zhu, *The Probability of Backtest Overfitting*](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf) and [Bailey and López de Prado, *The Deflated Sharpe Ratio*](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf). They motivate explicit treatment of selection and repeated testing; no implementation or replication of either paper is claimed here.

The reusable screen is: state the mechanism, required lawful data, target horizon, costs, leakage risks, sample/selection concerns and a falsifier; then classify the idea as educational, toy replication, serious candidate, parked or rejected. X2 did not reach the serious-candidate gate.

This note is newly written. Internal review chatter, stale main-project references, paper text and parked private dossiers are excluded. Any future independent work needs a specific feasible question and new evidence; dormancy creates no obligation to continue.

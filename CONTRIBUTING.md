# Contributing independently

Start with both offline quickstarts and `python scripts/verify_offline.py`. Use synthetic data and mocked services in tests. Preserve the distinction between an engineering fix and a changed strategy or research hypothesis.

For a change, explain the triggering problem, resulting behavior, relevant assumptions and focused validation. Add regression coverage when imports, data identity, session boundaries, costs, persistence or fail-closed decisions change. Never weaken a rejection or rewrite a historical outcome to make a test pass.

Research changes need an independent experiment record, lawful inputs, a complete trial ledger, a falsifier and a separately defined holdout. A promising example or a relative survivor is not a validated strategy. Keep exploratory work separate from the frozen research record.

Do not contribute third-party datasets, code snippets, author correspondence or papers without explicit redistribution permission and attribution. Do not submit private account details. Contributions need compatible rights under MIT and any required attribution. The included source is licensed on the basis of the owner's good-faith rights attestation; that attestation does not clear future contributions or third-party inputs.

This handoff promises no continuing maintenance. Independent forks and community maintenance are welcome under MIT. The source license does not authorize operations on anyone's accounts, deployments or schedulers.

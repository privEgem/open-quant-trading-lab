# Source and rights

This release is a curated extraction of original-project implementation/research sources plus newly written packaging, synthetic examples, tests and explanations. Exact private repository names, source paths and commit IDs are retained in a separate private review manifest. Public provenance uses component labels and release-file hashes, not private history.

Version 0.1.0 uses the standard MIT text in [LICENSE](../LICENSE), with Copyright (c) 2026 privEgem. The public display identity is privEgem and the repository name is privEgem/open-quant-trading-lab; naming it does not assert repository availability.

The owner attests, to the best of their knowledge, that they have the rights to publish and MIT-license the included material, with no known third-party, contributor, employer or contractual restrictions. This is a good-faith owner attestation, not an independently audited chain of title or legal opinion. Inspection did not find an effective upstream source LICENSE. Repository possession, Git authorship, scan results and absence of a license do not independently establish ownership. The private evidence retains per-file provenance and distinguishes technical inspection from the owner's factual attestation. No component was removed to bypass rights review.

No third-party research code or data is included. Journal replication archives, author-supplied R/RData, research PDFs, original empirical tables, broker exports, market bars and historical log packets are excluded. Academic notes are fresh factual summaries with links, not copies of papers or internal correspondence. Synthetic fixtures confer no right to use a provider's real data.

Dependencies are declared, not vendored. Check their terms before redistributing a wheelhouse, container or binary distribution. This source release does not resolve the rights of a future distribution format.

The [arXiv API terms](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/tou.html), consulted during source preparation, distinguish descriptive metadata from copyrighted e-prints and require aggregate rate-limit compliance. The included defaults use one sequential client with at least three seconds between requests. No online request was made to validate intake; future users must review current terms and individual content licenses. No endorsement or general right to redistribute abstracts/papers is claimed.

Each manifest entry records inclusion/reworking, provenance category, transformation, SHA-256 identity and owner-attested MIT rights scope. Schema version 1 and the self-hash convention are retained. The distribution descriptor `publication_status: LICENSED_SOURCE_RELEASE` describes the licensed source artifact; it asserts neither an upload nor publication approval. `technical_status: VALIDATION_RECORDED` points to historical observed checks, not certification. The `intended_repository` field is a name, not an existence assertion. The `publication_gates` array is empty because volatile approval and configuration state belongs in private operational evidence; emptiness grants no publication permission. Only the manifest's own SHA-256 is omitted internally; the external complete inventory and archive companions bind its exact bytes. Excluded source files are enumerated privately with reasons.
